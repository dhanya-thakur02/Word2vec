QUICK REFERENCE

Word2Vec learns a vector for every word from its neighbours

Word embedding

A word turned into a vector (a list of numbers). Similar words get similar vectors.

cat → (0.2, 0.7, …)
CBOW

Predicts the centre word from its context words.

the, cat, on, a → sat
Skip-gram

Predicts the context words from the centre word.

sat → the, cat, on, a

The window size w is how many words on each side of the centre word count as context. Examples here are lowercase and have no punctuation.

FORMULA

Two formulas to know

context words ≤ 2 × w

w is the window size. There are fewer context words near the start or end of a sentence.

cosine similarity = (a · b) ÷ (|a| × |b|)

It ranges from −1 (opposite) to 1 (same direction). Example: a = (1, 0) and b = (1, 1) give 1 ÷ (1 × 1.41) = 0.71.
